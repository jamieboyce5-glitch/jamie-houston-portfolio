Case 01 / WORK180 · Product Design · 2025
WORK180: Onboarding

Users were only prompted to sign up at high-intent moments, meaning most never discovered the value of following employers — limiting personalisation and repeat engagement.

I redesigned the onboarding journey to introduce account creation earlier, surface employer value upfront, and guide users to relevant employers through personalised recommendations.

Result: Employer follows grew from 323 → 2,126 per month, with unique new follows up from 78 → 221 — exceeding the 20% MoM growth target.

Case 02 / WORK180 · Product Design · 2023
WORK180: Employer Portal

The existing portal couldn't support a new DEI assessment model or self-service needs — leaving employers dependent on Account Managers to manage profiles, complete assessments, and track progress.

I designed a scalable self-service portal with simplified workflows, structured step-by-step interactions, and clear progress tracking — validated through usability testing and workshops with employers and stakeholders.

Result: Employer profile launch time dropped from 180 → 14 days, enabling a fully self-service experience and significantly reducing Account Manager overhead.
