WORK180:
Onboarding

Introducing onboarding earlier in the user journey to personalise the experience sooner, guide users toward relevant employers, and drive a significant uplift in employer follows.

Role Lead Product Designer
Year 2025
Tools Figma · Hotjar · Jira
Overview

The challenge

This project centred on designing the onboarding experience for new account creation, with a clear objective: double monthly employer follows. Previously, sign-up prompts only appeared at high-intent moments, which led to low awareness of the benefits of following employers, slower personalisation, and weaker repeat engagement.

Research

Two critical blockers

Through early research, I discovered two critical blockers hurting WORK180's growth. These weren't just UX issues, they were business problems directly impacting follower growth.

Blocker 1
No onboarding
Our business goal was to increase followers, but research made it clear how confusing that journey was. Users were prompted to log in at high-intent moments, with no guidance or context, leaving users with low awareness of the value of following employers and no path to personalisation.
Blocker 2
No explanation of value
There was no real explanation of what following an employer would do, or how WORK180 differs from a regular jobs board. Introducing onboarding would let us guide users and give them a clear understanding of how to get the most from their account and also prompt them to start following employers that matched what they we're looking for.
Process

How I approached it

As the Lead Designer, I led the redesign from discovery through to implementation, collaborating closely with Product, Engineering, Marketing, and our DEI advisory team.

Discovery
Used insights from Hotjar recordings, surveys, past research, and support data to identify why users weren't following employers earlier in their journey.
Design Workshops
Facilitated cross-functional sessions to identify opportunities and define high-impact, low-effort solutions.
User Flows & Wireframes
Mapped simplified journeys that introduced onboarding earlier and guided users toward following relevant employers.
UI Design
Created a clean, accessible, responsive design using WORK180's design system, highlighting employer recommendations and reinforcing the value of following.
Collaboration & QA
Worked closely with engineering to ensure smooth implementation, QA, and launch readiness.
Content Review
Refined onboarding copy to clearly communicate the benefits of follows and drive action.

The challenge
We identified an opportunity to help young people find the right apprenticeship path with full transparency, while giving employers a way to manage applications on the other side. The tricky part was that it's a two-sided product with very different user needs, and we jumped straight into it as an experiment with almost no runway. I had a week to show a working prototype to a potential client, starting from scratch.

What I did
I ran discovery and scoped the project myself, mapping out both sides of the experience simultaneously. I had to make fast, pragmatic calls about what to explore and what to park, and built the prototype end-to-end to get something tangible in front of stakeholders quickly.

How it evolved
The whole process has been learning while building, taking feedback from research and changing direction based on what we found rather than following a fixed plan. That's been the real design challenge: staying structured enough to make progress while staying loose enough to respond to what we learn.

Where it landed
We're currently in validation with some strong early signals. It hasn't shipped yet but the process itself validated a lot of our assumptions about what both sides of the market actually need.
