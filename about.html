A bit about me

Designer, problem-solver,
and lover of a good coffee

I'm Jamie Houston — a UX and product designer based in Melbourne, originally from New Zealand. I'm passionate about creating intuitive, user-centred experiences where thoughtful design makes complex problems feel simple.

With a Bachelor of Communication Design and 8 years across freelance, in-house, and startup environments, I've built a multi-disciplinary practice that spans product strategy, UX research, UI design, branding, and illustration. I care deeply about craft — not just how things look, but how they work and feel.

Background

Classically trained in communication design, I've spent 8 years honing a practice that goes wide — across digital product, brand, illustration, and marketing — and deep in UX and product design. I'm at my best at the intersection of rigour and creativity.

I believe great design comes from asking the right questions, not jumping to solutions. I bring structure to ambiguity, involve teams early, and move between strategic thinking and detailed craft with ease.

What I stand for
