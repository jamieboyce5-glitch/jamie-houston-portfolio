A bit about me

Designer, problem-solver,
and lover of a good coffee

I'm Jamie Houston, a UX and product designer based in Melbourne. I'm passionate about creating intuitive, user-centred experiences where thoughtful design makes complex problems feel simple.

With a Bachelor of Communication Design and 10 years across freelance, in-house, and startup environments, I've built a multi-disciplinary practice that spans product strategy, UX research, UI design, branding, and illustration.

Originally from New Zealand, I now call Melbourne home, where I live with my partner and daughter. Outside of design, you can find me outdoors exploring, discovering new places to eat, and enjoying a good coffee.

How I work

Ship early, learn along the way

Perfect is too late. Early feedback keeps things moving and stops us solving the wrong problem. Put it in front of users, learn fast, iterate.

Shape the process around the work

Every project is different. I stay flexible in how I work, choosing an approach that fits the challenge rather than following a fixed process.

Great work happens together

I value collaboration and open thinking. Sharing ideas early and often helps create better, more considered work.

I'll push back respectfully

Honest conversations lead to stronger work. I'll question things thoughtfully and bring solutions, not just criticism.

Balance user needs and business goals

I care about creating experiences that work for people and deliver real value for the business. The best solutions consider both.

Design for everyone

Accessibility isn't a layer, it's the baseline. If it doesn't work for everyone, it doesn't work.
